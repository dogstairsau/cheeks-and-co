Plenty of agencies call themselves independent. Fewer can tell you what it changes about the advice you get.

## Where the pressure usually comes from

Most media recommendations carry someone else's interests. A network deal that needs volume. A platform partnership with a spend commitment. A holding company with targets that have nothing to do with your business.

None of that is necessarily sinister. It just means the plan you're handed has been shaped by something other than your results.

## What changes without it

Being independent means we can say the unpopular thing:

- That the channel you're excited about won't work for your audience
- That your budget is better spent on fewer things, done properly
- That the campaign you're running should stop

We have no volume to hit and no partner to keep happy. The only thing our recommendation has to serve is whether it moves the needle for you.

## The practical test

Ask any agency this: what have you talked a client *out of* in the last six months?

If they can't answer quickly and specifically, the independence is decorative.
