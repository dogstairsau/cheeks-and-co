## The brief

The Grange Golf Club needed membership growth, but the starting point wasn't a campaign — it was a question. Who is actually joining, and why?

## What we did

We took the time to understand the Club, its audience and its goals before proposing a single placement, then translated that into a clear, effective strategy across digital advertising and targeted media.

- Audience profiling against real membership data
- Positioning built around the Club's genuine draw, not generic golf messaging
- Performance-driven digital campaigns with a transparent reporting dashboard
- Ongoing optimisation against lead quality, not just lead volume

## The result

The volume of leads generated exceeded expectations — so much so that the Club couldn't keep up.
